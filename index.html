Hi, I'm Sandy.

I'm an Australian geneticist doing a PhD at the University of Cambridge, where I combine genomic surveillance and mathematical modelling to inform public health responses to emerging mosquito-borne disease in East Africa. Outside the lab, I've co-founded a not-for-profit coffee company and the Cambridge Biosecurity Hub, a not-for-profit that runs programmes to help experienced researchers and skilled PhDs move into important biosecurity work. I write here about science, building things, and whatever I'm curious about at the moment.
